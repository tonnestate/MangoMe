# Code of Conduct

MangoMe is an open technical project. Participation is welcome from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, tools, employers, and viewpoints.

## Expected conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas, code, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking people;
- communicate clearly and professionally, especially during disagreement or review;
- give specific, reproducible technical criticism where possible;
- respect privacy, credentials, unpublished security information, and other people's work;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, or sustained personal abuse;
- disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect a review or decision;
- follow the repository's security process for vulnerabilities instead of publishing exploitable details prematurely.

Technical disagreement is not misconduct. Strong criticism of architecture, implementation, tests, or evidence is allowed and useful when it remains focused on the work.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to MangoMe repository spaces and to project-related interactions where someone is acting as a MangoMe contributor, maintainer, or representative.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer through an appropriate private channel rather than escalating publicly when privacy is needed. Security vulnerabilities should follow `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or block contributors when necessary to protect the project or its participants. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and documented privately when appropriate.

## Enforcement principles

Enforcement should be consistent, evidence-based, and focused on restoring a workable technical community. Good-faith mistakes should normally be corrected before stronger measures are used; serious or repeated abuse may justify immediate restriction.
